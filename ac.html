Asteroid City

★★★☆☆   1h 45min | Comedy, Drama, Romance

Plot

A fictional American desert town circa 1955. Junior Stargazers and Space Cadets from across the country assemble for the annual Asteroid Day celebration but the scholarly competition is spectacularly upended by world‑changing events.

Cast

Jason Schwartzman, Scarlett Johansson, Tom Hanks, Jeffrey Wright, Tilda Swinton, Bryan Cranston, Edward Norton, Adrien Brody, Liev Schreiber, Hope Davis, Rupert Friend, Maya Hawke, Steve Carell, Matt Dillon, Hong Chau, Willem Dafoe, Margot Robbie, Tony Revolori, Jake Ryan, and Jeff Goldblum.

Awards

Best Art Direction/Production Design from the Florida Film Critics Circle, Set Decorators Society of America Award for Best Achievement in Decor/Design of a Comedy or Musical Feature Film, Nominations for the 2023 Cannes Film Festival Palme d’Or.
