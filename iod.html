Isle of Dogs

★★★★☆   1h 41min | Animation, Adventure, Comedy

Plot

In a future Japan, a boy searches Trash Island for his lost dog and teams up with a group of exiled dogs to save them and uncover a conspiracy.

Cast

Bryan Cranston as Chief, Koyu Rankin as Atari Kobayashi, Edward Norton as Rex, Bill Murray as Boss, Jeff Goldblum as Duke, Greta Gerwig as Nutmeg, Scarlett Johansson as Spots, Frances McDormand as Oracle.

Awards

Silver Bear for Best Director at the Berlin International Film Festival. Nominated for Best Animated Feature, Best Original Score at the Academy Awards in 2019.
